Return & Exchange Policy

We aim to ensure that every product you receive meets your expectations. In case of any issues, please review our policy below.

Eligibility for Exchange

Exchanges are allowed under the following conditions:

  • Product received is damaged or defective
  • Incorrect product delivered
  • Size does not fit

You must raise a request within 7 days of delivery by emailing us at support@invogue.shop or through the exchange portal.

How to Request an Exchange

To initiate an exchange, share your Order ID along with the reason for exchange. Our team will review your request within 24–48 hours and guide you through the next steps.

Once approved:

  1. A reverse pickup will be scheduled.
  2. The product will undergo a quality check upon return.
  3. The replacement product will be dispatched after approval.

Exchange Charges

A nominal fee of ₹100 is applicable for exchanges to cover logistics costs. This includes reverse pickup and re-delivery.

In case reverse pickup is unavailable in your area, you may self-ship the product. The exchange fee will be waived/refunded in such cases.

Size Exchange

If the requested size is unavailable, you may:

  • Wait for restock, or
  • Choose an alternative product/variant

Refund Policy

We do not offer direct monetary refunds for delivered products.

In exceptional cases (such as product unavailability or approved special requests), refunds may be issued in the form of a store credit or discount code of equivalent value within 3–7 working days.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

All orders are processed via Cash on Delivery. Payment is collected only at the time of delivery.

Order Cancellation

Orders can only be cancelled before they are confirmed or shipped. Once the order has been processed or dispatched, cancellation will not be possible.

To cancel an order, please contact us immediately after placing it.

For any further queries, feel free to reach out at support@invogue.shop.
