Shipping & Payment Policy

We offer reliable and timely delivery across all serviceable locations. All orders are processed promptly and typically delivered within 3–5 working days.

Once your order is placed, you will receive updates at every stage — including order confirmation, dispatch details, and delivery status. Please ensure you check your email or notifications regularly for updates.

In certain cases due to logistics or high demand, delivery may take up to 7–10 working days. We always strive to deliver your order as quickly as possible.

Payment Method (Cash on Delivery)

We currently offer Cash on Delivery (COD) as our primary payment method. This means you only pay for your order at the time of delivery.

Please ensure that the correct amount is available at the time of delivery to avoid any inconvenience.

Order Cancellation

Orders can only be cancelled before they are confirmed or shipped. Once an order has been processed or dispatched, cancellation will not be possible.

If you wish to cancel your order, please contact support at the earliest after placing the order.
