Shipping & Payment Policy

We currently deliver across Maharashtra only. Every order is carefully processed and packed to ensure quality and timely delivery.

Shipping & Delivery

Standard delivery time is 7 days from the date of order confirmation.

In some cases, due to logistics delays or high order volume, delivery may take up to 10–12 days.

You will receive real-time order updates directly on the website at every stage, including: order confirmation, dispatch, and delivery status.

Invoice

Once your payment is successfully received, the invoice will be sent to your registered email address.

Payment Method

We currently offer Cash on Delivery (COD) as our payment method.

  • Payment is collected at the time of delivery
  • Please keep the exact amount ready for smooth delivery

Order Cancellation

Orders cannot be cancelled once they are confirmed or processed. If you wish to cancel an order, you must contact support immediately after placing it. Once the order is shipped or under processing, cancellation will not be possible.
